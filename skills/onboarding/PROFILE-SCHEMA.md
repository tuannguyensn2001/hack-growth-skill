# Learner profile schema

The template for `profile.md`. Every skill in this suite reads this file, so keep the headings exactly as written. Fill values in the learner's language.

Field values are one of:

- an answer,
- a rating with its evidence,
- `unknown (<reason>)`, where the reason is `declined`, `unsure`, or `deferred`.

## Template

```markdown
# Learner profile

Created: YYYY-MM-DD · Updated: YYYY-MM-DD

## Identity
- Name / nickname:
- Conversation language:
- Background: <occupation, education, relevant life context>

## Goals
### North star
<long-term outcome, 1+ years out, and why it matters to the learner>

### Near-term goals
| Goal | Measurable outcome | Deadline | Why now |
|------|--------------------|----------|---------|

## Domains
<!-- One block per domain the learner is studying. -->
### <Domain name>
- Why this domain:
- Sub-skills:

| Sub-skill | Level (Dreyfus) | Evidence | Source |
|-----------|-----------------|----------|--------|

<!-- Source: probe | evidence | self-reported -->

- Strengths:
- Weaknesses:
- Past attempts: <what they tried before, what worked, why they stopped>
- Resources already owned: <courses, books, subscriptions, mentors>

## Constraints
- Time per week:
- Usual study times / session length:
- Budget for paid resources:
- Devices and environment:
- Hard dates: <exams, interviews, launches>

## Learning preferences
- Formats that work: <reading, video, hands-on projects, spaced repetition, discussion>
- Formats that don't:
- Feedback style: <blunt, encouraging, Socratic>
- Depth vs breadth:
- Motivation and accountability: <what keeps them going, what made them quit before>

## Open questions
<anything to revisit in a later session>

## Changelog
- YYYY-MM-DD: <what changed and why>
```

## Dreyfus scale, applied to a sub-skill

| Level | The learner… |
|-------|--------------|
| Novice | follows rules and recipes; stuck when the recipe doesn't fit |
| Advanced beginner | recognises common situations; still needs guidance on the unfamiliar |
| Competent | plans and solves typical problems alone; slow on novel ones |
| Proficient | sees the whole situation intuitively; deliberates only on the details |
| Expert | acts fluently, handles novel cases, can teach and extend the field |
