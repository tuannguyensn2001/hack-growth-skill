# Shape of a step

## Template

```
Step n/N: <title>

<headline: the idea in one sentence>

<body>

📍 In the source: <section / page / timestamp>
↪ Questions, or next? (Go deeper: <the one detail worth zooming into>)
```

## The body at the right altitude

The body carries the idea whole and nothing below the step's altitude:

- **Show, then tell**: lead with a concrete example (input/output, a small diagram, a trace) chosen as in the `explain` skill's `SHOW.md`, then state the idea. For software and technical topics, this is the default.
- **Why it matters**: one sentence linking the idea to the previous step or to the learner's goal.
- **Fits on one screen**, roughly 100–250 words plus the example. Longer means the step holds two ideas, or detail from below its altitude.

The altitude test, line by line: would the learner misunderstand the idea without this line? Yes → keep it. No → it is detail, and the best candidate goes in the "Go deeper" offer; the rest stays in the source.

## Coverage

Concise never means incomplete. Every claim the step's idea depends on appears in the body, even if in a single clause; what gets cut is elaboration, repetition, and edge cases the idea doesn't hinge on.
